# Privacy and tracking limits

Image and link endpoints store request timestamps, delivery/link identifiers, parsed browser family and versioned proxy/scanner classification. They do not store raw IPs, full user-agent strings, geolocation or browser fingerprints. Capabilities use random 32-byte tokens; only SHA-256 token hashes are stored. The new event stream captures rapid repeated requests up to 10,000 observations per delivery, then marks coverage incomplete. The legacy stream still combines requests within five minutes and caps at 1,000; imported history is labeled legacy and is not reconstructed as raw history.

Requests are not verified reads or people. Google/Apple can cache, proxy or prefetch images, and scanners can follow links. Five-minute estimated sessions group requests per delivery and event kind; known proxies/scanners are excluded, but remaining requests are not verified humans. Shared sends and forwarded links retain aggregate delivery attribution. Recipient inbox/spam placement is unknown. Add-on preparations do not confirm sending.

Hosted PDFs, JPEGs and PNGs are private objects. Immutable uploads remain until owner deletion, which revokes all shares immediately and queues object deletion within an hour. Share expiry stops access; it does not delete the original. Sessions expire after at most an hour and use HttpOnly/Secure cookies. The viewer records loaded sessions, pages exposed for at least a second, approximate visible foreground time (pauses after 30 seconds idle), and download requests. It cannot observe downloaded-file use, guarantee attention, authenticate a recipient, or prevent screenshots. Forwarding a share forwards access.

The owner may separately grant Gmail read access. Sync stores only matched tracked-thread headers, snippets, direction and timestamps. Full incoming bodies are inspected transiently for matching/DSN parsing and are not stored. Own messages and obvious automated replies are excluded. Polling uses Gmail history with a bounded 30-day recovery window. Disconnecting removes the encrypted refresh token and mailbox data; pausing stops sync without revoking the Google grant.

Goals and outcomes retain source, event time, optional monetary value/currency and reversals. Signed webhook events without eligible click evidence remain unattributed. Manual outcomes are distinct from provider evidence. Detailed activity is retained until parent data deletion; automatic 90-day pruning and durable rollups are not enabled. Database and private bucket backups require operator retention controls. Native composer attachments retain their existing short-lived cleanup behavior.

No central relay or third-party viewer analytics are used. Caddy has no access log configured, and application error logs redact request paths. Infrastructure providers or operator-added proxies may keep their own network/request logs. Operators should communicate tracking to recipients and manage access, backups and retention for their instance.
